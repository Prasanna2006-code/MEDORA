MEDORA

MEDORA is an AI-assisted emergency triage and patient prioritization prototype designed to support healthcare professionals in rapidly organizing emergency cases.

The system uses patient symptoms, vital signs, medical history, pain score, GCS score and arrival mode to generate an urgency category and priority score.

MEDORA is a decision-support prototype and does not replace professional clinical judgment or diagnosis.
